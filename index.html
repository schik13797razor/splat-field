    The basics
    • You're the player with the white ring. Two bots play on your team, three on the other.
    • Every paintball that hits someone is a point for your team.
    • Got hit? Walk to your team's WASH pad and stand on it for 3 seconds to get back in. It refills your paint too. (In Last Team Standing, one hit and you're out for the round.)
    • Your hopper runs dry: watch the paint meter and press R in a quiet moment. Sprinting uses stamina and you can't shoot while you do it.
    • Squad orders for your two bots: 1 follow me, 2 hold the spot under your crosshair, 3 attack, 4 back to normal.
